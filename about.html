About The Trust

Where Compassion
Meets Spirituality

"Service to Man is Service to God."

Sai Shankara Peetha Charitable Trust was established with a divine mandate: to bridge the gap between ancient spiritual wisdom and modern social responsibility. Headquartered in Bangalore, our organization is dedicated to uplifting the forgotten, feeding the hungry, and healing the sick.

Guided by the philosophy of Adi Shankara and the compassion of Shirdi Sai Baba, we believe that true devotion is expressed through selfless action.

  • Medical Relief: Healing the sick through free camps.
  • Annadhanam: Ensuring no one sleeps hungry.
  • Spiritual Heritage: Reviving the Kudajadri legacy.

The Founder's Message

Words from the heart of our mission

"Our mission is simple yet profound: to serve humanity with the same devotion with which we worship God. Whether it is building a temple at Kudajadri or feeding a destitute elder, every act is an offering. We invite you to join hands with us in this sacred journey of 'Seva' and 'Sadhana'."

Mr. Sai Prasad Manikoth

Founder & Managing Trustee

Our Core Values

The Pillars of Sai Shankara Peetha

Compassion

We believe in empathy not just as a feeling, but as an action. Seeing God in every living being is the foundation of our service.

Integrity

Transparency in every donation received and every rupee spent. We hold ourselves accountable to our donors and the divine.

Wisdom

Promoting the timeless teachings of the Vedas, Yoga, and our great Gurus to enlighten minds and purify hearts.
