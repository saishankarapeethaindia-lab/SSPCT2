Nestled in the serene foothills of the sacred Kudajadri Hills, our Old Age Home is not just a shelter—it is a sanctuary of love, dignity, and care. Here, the golden years are not measured in days, but in smiles, shared stories, and the gentle warmth of companionship.

"In this quiet corner of nature, where the breeze carries the whispers of ancient forests... we have created a space that feels like home—because it is one."

Our Core Services & Facilities

Old Age Home

A dignified living space where elders are treated as family, filled with respect and comfort.

Medical Support

Regular medical checkups and assistance to ensure the well-being of all residents.

Annadanam

Providing nutritious food to devotees and the needy as a sacred duty.

Spiritual Life

Blessed by Kudajadri Temple View, Bhadrakali Temple, and Naga Prathista.
