Spiritual Center

Construction and maintenance of a Shirdi Saibaba Prayer Hall and installation of the Adishankara Statue to promote spiritual well-being.

Medical Relief

Establishing dispensaries, organizing health camps, and providing medical aid to the poor and needy in rural areas.

Education Support

Running schools and colleges, and providing scholarships, grants, and uniforms to deserving students irrespective of caste or creed.

Annadhanam

Providing free food (Annadhanam) to the poor, devotees, and the hungry, ensuring no one goes to sleep on an empty stomach.

Old Age Homes

Establishing and maintaining homes for the aged and destitute, providing them with shelter, food, and medical care.

Yoga & Ayurveda

Promoting ancient Indian sciences like Yoga and Ayurveda, and establishing study centers for Sanskrit and Vedas.

Sai Shankara Peetha

A Charitable Trust dedicated to serving humanity through various social, educational, and spiritual initiatives. Join us in making a difference.
