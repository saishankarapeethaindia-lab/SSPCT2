Who We Are

Service to Humanity is Service to God

Sai Shankara Peetha Charitable Trust was established with a vision to uplift the underprivileged through holistic service.

We focus on providing educational support, medical relief to the needy, and spiritual nourishment to the community. Our trust believes in the transformative power of 'Annadhanam' and selfless service.
