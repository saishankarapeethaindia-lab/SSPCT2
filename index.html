Shirdi Sai Deepa Mandiram

Proposed Kerala-Style Spiritual Complex & Pilgrim Support Center

Execution Phase 1-Year Roadmap

Integrating Sacred Illumination & Public Service

Situated on a premier 40-cent property directly opposite the Mookambika Bus Stand in Kollur, the proposed Mandiram incorporates the traditional Kerala Mathruka architectural style. The centerpiece of the complex is its philosophy of Jyotir Aaradhana—continuous illumination powered by an Akhanda Jyoti and a brass Chuttuvilakku network, acting as a living symbol of the elimination of hunger, illness, and social neglect.

Continuous Annadaanam

Non-commercial, daily free meal distribution networks dedicated to visiting pilgrims and economically vulnerable families.

Emergency First Aid

Dedicated emergency first-response check points, resting rooms, and sanitation facilities to assist distressed travelers.

Humanitarian Outreach

Structured social welfare campaigns and local community support, subject to direct governance and statutory audit.

Institutional Accountability Statement: All financial allocations, land transfers, public contributions, and local welfare expenditures are maintained under strict internal accounting controls, in full compliance with the provisions of the 1920 Trust Act, and overseen by the Trust's designated Statutory Auditor, CA Amith M.
